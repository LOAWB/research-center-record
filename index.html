  • Since MP was built: MP (then FVAC) at 14.20, the simple average of four CIBC buy confirmations (15 Sep 2020 at 15.70, 5 Oct at 14.00, 11 Nov at 13.094, 16 Nov at 14.025; quantities redacted), confirmed by Louis. That MP was about 50% of the book and that the rest of the book rose with it until March 2021 is Louis's statement; no posts or statements cover the non-MP half for 2020.
  • Posted record: a daily model of every posted trade. Starting weights on 19 March 2021 are estimated from Louis's Chat notes (MP about 50%, ARKG 12%, PRNT 8%, RKLB 4%, smaller holdings, about 18% cash). Other reasonable starting weights and exit assumptions give a range of +34.6% to +111.3%.

  • Cash earns 0%. Forex, index CFDs, crypto and the 2021 Lockheed park are excluded. Dividends are included for the portfolio (paid into cash) and for SPY and QQQ (reinvested).

5. Clarifications from Louis

Where the channel alone did not settle the numbers, Louis answered directly on 25 September 2026. Everything else on this page comes from his posts (announcements, plus the Chat items marked "Chat"), except market prices and SPAC merger dates.

  • RKLB: beyond the posted adds (about 18% of book), the rest of the 50% in #357 is price growth.
  • MP 45% (#324) and PYPL 20% (#337): no fill on record. Louis: assume mid-range, about -23%. The page uses the midpoint of each exit day's own market range, which gives MP -22.7% and PYPL -24.9% (PYPL traded -24.2% to -25.5% that day, so -23% was not reachable).
  • Lockheed park (#17, 2021): the money was parked in LMT instead of held as cash and, as the original post said, not counted toward the portfolio. Excluded from all calculations.
  • The 10 edited trade posts: no edit changed a size or a price.
  • Forex and crypto trades: excluded from all calculations for now, even though they added to overall returns.
  • PRNT and ARKG: closed on 2 July 2026 with the #357 instruction to drop small positions. Use the middle of that day's market range (PRNT 24.43, ARKG 43.10), to be realistic for members who needed time to close.
  • TEM: 59.31 is the accurate average. URA: the position was opened across several accounts, with currency conversion and settlement, so the posted average (29.16) and the fills (31.47) differ slightly; use the midpoint, 30.32.
  • Posts #8, #31, #96, #245, #254 and #358 to #362 (missing from the export): likely typos or accidental messages.
  • BABA: the "final 2.5%" add planned on 2024-11-20 did not happen, so #322 closing 47.5% is the full position.
  • FLT and ACHR: closed at breakeven. SPCE and MNTS: count as -100% (small positions). On SPCE, members were told several times to take partial profits while it was up, so Louis puts the real outcome closer to breakeven.
  • FLT was not still held when Drone Delivery Canada merged with Volatus Aerospace in 2024.
